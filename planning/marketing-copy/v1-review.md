# V1 Copy Review

## What readers take away

### Home page

teamfluence Pro turns engagement with LinkedIn content into qualified leads for content agencies. It helps agencies start conversations, qualify and enrich leads with workflows and AI, manage replies in the teambox, and deliver leads while keeping control of the process. The page also presents teamfluence as a platform for managing multiple clients, with white-label branding, separate workspaces, access controls, onboarding, and a price that grows with the agency's client base.

### Entire Pro content

The full site explains the process in more detail: choose influencers and keywords, track relevant posts, collect people in a lead funnel, qualify them, start conversations, deliver qualified leads, and learn which content produced the most promising results. The other pages describe the available tools, explain how agencies manage clients, and present onboarding as four guided sessions from briefing to post-launch review.

## Writing style

The writing is direct, simple, and focused on what the user can do. It uses short sentences and concrete product descriptions, with technical terms where the product requires them. The copy is practical and feature-led rather than promotional.

## Emotional state

The copy leaves a feeling of control, competence, and support. AI and automation reduce manual work, but the agency remains responsible for decisions and client delivery. The tone is calm and operational; the unfinished “Clean neighbourhood” section means the reassurance around policies and account safety is not yet part of the message.

## Points for discussion

- The copy currently names content agencies as the customer while much of the core content-to-lead message also speaks to content marketers, so we should discuss whether the positioning remains agency-first or addresses both groups.
- Pricing must be finalized.
- The policy page has not been started and requires more input.
