# Risks

## High

- We are depending on one single tool: LinkedIn
- Competitors are cheaper and promise better results for less money.
- We are unable to demonstrate that focused content leads to better leads.

## Low

**External costs**

To deliver premium features like enrichment and ICP matching, we will require external resources. The cost may not be covered by a monthly flat fee, so we must define safeguards to avoid subsidizing power users.

**Is our client base too low?**

With the latest pricing model, starting at 599/month, we have a target of 100 base subscriptions. We treat direct customers like agencies, which opens teamfluence immediately to our client base.  

**Will higher end customers request more specific features tailored to their needs?**

Our focus must be clearly defined and communicated:

→ Focus on content → collect signals → automate selection → manual quality control and adjustments → Validate content that drove best results → Adjust content strategy

Everything that helps improving this cycle can be an addition to the road map
