# Dictionary

### Agency or content agency

Our first target group: boutique content agencies that manage content and lead generation for multiple clients.

### Marketers

People who work for content agencies or direct clients.

### Customers

Customers pay teamfluence.

### Clients

Clients pay our customers.

### People

People are the results of relevant signals in teamfluence. They are not leads by default. Workflows or manual decisions can turn people into leads.

Use **people** when referring to this group. Do not use **persons**.

### Contact

Use **contact** when referring to one individual record, as in HubSpot. Do not use **person** as the product term for a single record.

### Lead

A profile from People that has entered the lead funnel through a workflow or a manual decision.

### Qualified lead

A lead that has passed all filters and meets all criteria, and is ready to be handed over to the client. It can become a qualified sales opportunity or a sales-qualified lead (SQL).

### Signal

An activity that teamfluence uses to find people. Signals are useful when explaining how the product works, but we do not emphasize the term in headlines or taglines.

### Target

Use **target**, not **goal**, for a specific metric the customer wants to reach.

### teamfluence

Write **teamfluence** in lowercase, including at the beginning of a sentence. Use **teamfluence Pro** for the product name.

### teambox

The shared inbox for client conversations. Write **teambox** in lowercase, including at the beginning of a sentence.

### Onboarding sessions

Onboarding consists of four client meetings or touchpoints:

1. Briefing
2. Training and best practices
3. Workspace setup
4. Post-launch review

Monitoring takes place during the first weeks after launch and is discussed in the post-launch review.

### Signals and Platform

Two sections in the application:

- Signals is the operational part of the application, where users manage content, the funnel, and the teambox.
- Platform contains the main setup and process configuration. It includes workspace setup, workflow configuration, networking campaigns, and other configuration sections.
- A third section may become the administrative area for billing, access rules, and related settings.
