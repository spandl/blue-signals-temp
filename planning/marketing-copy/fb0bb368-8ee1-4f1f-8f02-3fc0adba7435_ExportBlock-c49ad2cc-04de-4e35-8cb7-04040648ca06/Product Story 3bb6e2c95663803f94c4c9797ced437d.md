# Product Story

## The core idea

- ***Find leads from the signals your content creates***. Content you publish yourself, or content you track.
- Build a funnel with smart lists by applying rules and filters:
    - Filter by how many signals a person has to send before they count as a lead
    - Match people against your ICP profiles
    - Adjust the lists by hand at any point
- See which content brought in the best leads, and adjust your content strategy.

## The brand story

Teamfluence is a LinkedIn lead generator built for content marketers

We help you find the people who care about your topics and engage with your content. We tell the difference between noise and real interest.

You get a set of automation tools to find these people, but you keep the steering wheel. Teamfluence gives you a ***light CRM*** to move a person from a first raw signal, to a qualified lead, to a lead you hand over to your client. The list stays yours: you decide who is on it, and you have several ways to present it, so your client can see where every lead came from and how warm it is.

Teamfluence is also a learning tool. We show you which content produces real signals and which content only produces noise, so you can fine-tune your content strategy over time.
