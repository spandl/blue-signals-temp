# Product Feature Skeleton

- White Label
    - e.g.client don’t see Teamfluence
- Pre-CRM CRM
    - List View & Kanban View
    - Lead/People Lists
        - configure views (which columns, which attribute)
        - configure who can view (granular rights)
        - smart and static
    - Approval Process
        - who can see and do what?
    - Programatic Export (cron:send emails with configured exports)
- Unified Master inbox
    - Channels
    - who can impersonate whom?
- Content Analysis
    - What worked, what didn’t and with whom?
    - CRM→Lead→Close/Won→feedback (status: close/won)
    - AI Content Analysis
        - All posts that worked - what did they have in common?
        - for each list
