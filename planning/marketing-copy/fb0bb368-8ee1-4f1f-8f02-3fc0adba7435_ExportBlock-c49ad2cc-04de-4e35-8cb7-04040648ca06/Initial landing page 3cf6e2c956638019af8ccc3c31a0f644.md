# Initial landing page

## Content

What are the seven (7) features or selling points that a prospect should write down on a paper? Three of them they can keep in their head. 

What are the emotional and rational arguments behind each point? Which arguments lean more towards emotional or rational thinking?

### Top 7 promises

- Every LinkedIn signal can be a lead
- Best practice policies
- Automatically start a conversation

---

- IA supported, but human managed
- Content-focused lead generation cycle
- Optimized for content marketing agencies
- Full support for setup, easy operations.

### Arguments

#### Every LinkedIn signal can be a lead

- With teamfluence, I can capture leads based on interest in my business, but it captures also visits, connections and direct messages, all combined in one single UI
- I can organize them in a funnel, like in a CRM
- I have automation tools to master the funnel
- I can use ABM marketing with teamfluence

Emotional

- Excitement: There are so many signals, this tool helps me to get the leads

#### Best practice policies

- teamfluence makes everything to prevent my profiles being unsafe
- teamfluence is not selling the service to spammers and do take privacy and legal restrictions serious

Emotional

- Fear: Oh my good, I didn’t even know about all the risks with the competition

#### Automatically start a conversation

- I can save a lot of time, by using workflows and outreach campaigns
- I can automate certain tasks to filter immediately leads that don’t fit
- Unified inbox helps me monitor the process

Emotional

- Relief: I can automate a big part of my work and can take some work load from sales

#### IA supported, but human managed

Rational

- You control how much you trust IA and processes - you can validate every single lead that goes out to sales
- ICP matches is only one step in the selection process
- I can use a combination of techniques to retain a solid base of leads
- I have automated and manual enrichment options in my hand

Emotional

- Relief: Not another IA tool that sucks once you start using it.

#### Content-focused lead generation cycle

- I can use my content, but also content from influencers and competition to find leads
- Feedback cycle about content that works helps me improving my content strategy

Emotional

- Good fit: They really care about the subject, this is not a spam tool

#### Optimized for content marketers (or segmented LinkedIn strategies)

- White label
- Managing multiple clients in one place
- Unified inbox
- Fine-grained access control, so my clients only see what they should see
- Saving different views makes this tool work for different kind of clientele
- Different ways to export data makes this tool work for different kind of clientele
- Targets let me quickly see if things do not go as expected
- Better pricing for many seats

Emotional: 

- Trust: I can use this for my clients without loosing them
- I will be able to handle all my clients and scale the process

#### Full support for setup, easy operations.

- teamfluence offers great onboarding and help me setting up the hard part
- The interface for operations is easy and straightforward

Emotional

- There is a team of professionals behind teamfluence, people I can talk to and that solve my issues.
