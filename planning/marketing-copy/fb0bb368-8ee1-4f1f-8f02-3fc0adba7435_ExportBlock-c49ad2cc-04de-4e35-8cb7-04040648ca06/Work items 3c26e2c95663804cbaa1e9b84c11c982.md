## Mental Models

### The Pre-CRM-CRM

both end of the client spectrum pose challenges in regard to CRM-connectivity, because they

- either don’t have a CRM at all
- or have one that is tightly guarded

So Teamfluence should fill that gap as a Pre-CRM-CRM, that help with the workflow of generating, qualifying and approving CRMs before they are send to the CRM or even function as a de-facto CRM between the agency and their sales team.

### The Approval Workflow

Content production and ghost writing, at a certain maturity level, has typically some sort of approval process between ideation, creation, editing and approval and publishing. If we understand “the new product” to be a platform to support this workflow the acceptance of leads is a series of further workflow steps between the stakeholders.

e.g.

$strategy → ideation → content → approval → publication → engagement → qualification → crm$

## Feature inventory

### **I. AI Insights / Content Ideation** *(net new, highest ambition)*

- Structured content briefs (not copy) — what worked, why it worked, why it’s culturally relevant right now. Deliberately does not write the post itself; that stays the agency’s job.
- Three layers of analysis: (1) tag/metadata trends, (2) deep text/structure analysis of what converted, (3) external trend signal (Reddit, Google Trends, broader web).
- Folded-in influencer/keyword discovery: since the AI is already scanning for trending people/topics, it can propose new LinkedIn influencers/keywords to track, not leave that to manual research.
