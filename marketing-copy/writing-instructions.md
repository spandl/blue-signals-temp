# Writing instructions for teamfluence

## Overview
- We write in English
- For most of our customers, English is their second language
- We write simple English. We are sophisticated, but don't need to proof it. Everybody should understand, nobody be left out,
- We are bold and confident, but we don't translate that in bloated sentences. In the contrary, 
    - we are precise
    - we avoid marketing fill word
    - we avoid marketing buzz words and formulations
    - we have so much to say, that we say just that. To the point. No need to waste anybody's time
- In German, there is a magazine called Brand Eins. They write really well. The text flows, it is confident, thoughtful and delicate. Long sentences and short sentences give a rythm and at the end you don't even realize that you spent time reading the stuff
- Content first, fowrm follows
- While we are straight about how we formulate content, we are aware that emotions are important, but use with care.
