# Dictionary

### Agency or Content agency

The first group of customers we go after: Boutique style content agencies that manage social media campaigns on behalf their clients

### Marketers

People working for content agencies or direct clients

### Customers

We talk about customers, when they pay us

### Clients

We talk about clients when they pay our customers

### Target

We shall use the word target and not goal when it comes to reaching specific metrics

### Qualified lead

A lead that has passed all filters and meets all criteria, and is handed over to the client. In other words, a lead that can be converted into a qualified sales opportunity or a sales-qualified lead (SQL).

### Signals and Platform

Two sections in the application

- Signals is the operational part of the application, whereby the user manages content, the funnel and the inbox
- Platform is the base configuration, that is mainly setup in the beginning and adjusted by professional users. It contains workspace setup, workflow configuration, networking campaigns and other configuration sections
- A third section may become the administrative part, which controls, billing, access rules etc
