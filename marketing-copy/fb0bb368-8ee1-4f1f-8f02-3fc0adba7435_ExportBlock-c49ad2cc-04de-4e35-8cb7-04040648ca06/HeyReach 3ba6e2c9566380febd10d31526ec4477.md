# HeyReach

1. **Safety and Architecture Risks**

**Proxy Claims vs. Reality**: While HeyReach claims to use dedicated residential proxies, tests observed them using datacenter IPs with high fraud scores (100/100) and proxy/VPN flags. This can increase the risk of LinkedIn flagging your account.

**Cloud-Side Session Custody**: The extension uploads your entire LinkedIn cookie jar, and login credentials are sent to HeyReach's servers for server-side login. This means session custody lies with the vendor, not your local browser.

1. **Pricing and Plan Limitations**

**The "Unlimited" Cap:** Despite the name and fixed-cost framing, the Unlimited plan actually has a shared pool cap of 300 seats.

**Credit Reductions on Upgrades:** Moving to an agency tier can reduce your per-sender enrichment credits. For example, the Growth plan offers 100 credits per sender, but the Micro Agency plan (25 senders) only provides a total of 1,000 credits (averaging 40 per sender).

**White-label Friction:** White-labeling is not a self-serve switch; it is restricted to specific plans and requires going through customer support.

1. **Automation and Campaign Control Gaps**

**Rigid Workflow Branching:** The platform offers basic conditional flow control (e.g., If Connection, If Open Profile, Email Found), but it lacks open-ended custom triggers, nested conditions, or reply-based branching.

**Campaign Prioritization:** You cannot manually override campaign priorities. If a sender runs multiple campaigns, they share a daily action pool on a first-launched-wins basis.

**Manual Deduplication:** There is no automatic, organization-wide duplicate protection. Agencies must rely on manual "Exclude Lists" to prevent overlapping outreach across client workspaces.

1. **Target and Lead-Sourcing Limits**

**No Group Scraping:** HeyReach cannot scrape leads from LinkedIn group members.

**No Built-in Email Verification**: While the tool has a "Find Email" step, there is no standalone feature to verify existing email addresses you may have uploaded.

**Missing Engagement Actions:** You cannot automate specific engagement steps like "skill endorsements."

1. **AI and Integration Gaps**

**Narrow Native Integrations:** HeyReach only offers two true native CRM integrations (HubSpot and Breakcold). Popular CRMs like Salesforce and Pipedrive must be connected through Zapier. There are also no inbound webhooks.

**Limited AI Scope:** Despite having an official MCP server and a ChatGPT message generator, HeyReach lacks native AI ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) scoring and automated AI post-commenting.

**Reliability Issues:** Reviews are somewhat polarized, with tool reliability (including inconsistencies between the MCP server data and the main dashboard) being the most common structural complaint.
