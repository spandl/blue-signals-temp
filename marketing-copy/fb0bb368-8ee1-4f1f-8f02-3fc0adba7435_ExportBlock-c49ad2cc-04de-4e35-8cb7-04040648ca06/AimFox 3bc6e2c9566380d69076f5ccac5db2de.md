# AimFox

*(https://www.salesrobot.co/blogs/aimfox-review)*

**Compliance Risks and Potential Bans:** Renting LinkedIn profiles technically violates LinkedIn’s Terms of Service. If one of these rented accounts gets flagged or banned by LinkedIn, you lose all the connections and conversations associated with it.

**Steep Learning Curve:** Because it has advanced features like multi-profile management, rented accounts, and AI customization, the platform can be overwhelming for new users. You have to invest time upfront just to learn how to use it effectively.

***(https://useoutly.com/en/blog/aimfox-alternatives/)***

**Costs Escalate Quickly:** Each Outreach Avatar costs $129/month and is billed quarterly, not monthly. The Agency plan starts at $499/month for 20 seats. Teams adding multiple Avatars for volume will find the bill climbing quickly without a flexible exit option.

**Manual Reply Management:** The automation only covers outbound messaging. Once a prospect replies, you have to manage the conversation manually, which becomes a serious bottleneck when running campaigns across multiple profiles.
