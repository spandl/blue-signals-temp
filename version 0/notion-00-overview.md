# Overview

A new product concept, spun off from **Teamfluence** (teamfluence.com). Teamfluence today captures LinkedIn "signals" (profile visits, likes, comments, follows, connections) around a sales team's outbound activity and routes them into GTM/CRM workflows — "Engagement to Sales Pipeline."

This concept reuses Teamfluence's signal-capture and workflow engine, but **repositions it away from outbound sales prospecting and toward content performance and audience discovery** for high-end LinkedIn content programs. Instead of chasing prospects, the product finds the real people who already resonate with content you publish — and helps you tell the difference between a real signal and noise.

**Target customers** (deliberately high-end, no self-serve/solo tier):
1. **Beachhead** — boutique content/ghostwriting agencies managing LinkedIn content for multiple retainer clients (their clients can be smaller companies; we never sell directly to an agency's own clients).
2. **Volume segment** — 50+ employee B2B SaaS/tech companies with a real internal marketing/sales/CS structure, running an agency-like content operation around their executives.

Internally the project has been code-named **"Top 10%"** — not a literal brand name, but the philosophy: we'd rather find 10 real, high-quality matches than 1,000 low-quality contacts.
